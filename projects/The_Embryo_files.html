Research Vision

Can AI learn the language of life by observing how a single cell develops into an entire organism?

This research journal documents my exploration of developmental biology, computational biology, computer vision, and machine learning through real biological imaging datasets. Rather than presenting polished results, these notes capture the process of asking questions, testing ideas, understanding failures, and gradually building the knowledge required to investigate one of biology's most fascinating problems: reconstructing cellular development from microscopy data. The work is conducted as an independent research initiative.
