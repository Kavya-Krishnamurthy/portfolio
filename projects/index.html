About Me

Hi! Welcome 👋

So… this is the place where I keep track of the things I’ve learned, the things I’ve built, and the many moments where I thought, “How hard can this be?” (A question that has led to some very interesting learning experiences. 😄)

This website is my little corner of the internet for documenting projects, sharing what I’m learning, and writing about topics that capture my attention. I’ve always believed that curiosity is one of the best superpowers we have — the kind that makes you ask one more question, explore one more idea, and occasionally wonder why you didn’t discover something sooner.

My curiosity usually takes me somewhere between bioinformatics, computer science, research, and whatever fascinating idea has caught my attention lately. Here you’ll find my projects, research notes, coding adventures, and little discoveries along the way.

I don’t think learning is ever a perfectly straight path. It’s more like a fun little adventure filled with questions, “aha!” moments, and the occasional “okay… how does this even work?” before finally figuring it out.

I’ve intentionally kept the technology behind this website simple, so I can focus on what matters most: the ideas, the stories, the projects, and the learning process itself — rather than spending all my time debating tools and settings. (There are already enough things in life to overthink. 😄)

A lot of this curiosity is shared with my husband, my favorite brainstorming partner, who is just as excited about learning and exploring new ideas as I am. Together, we are the kind of people who can start with a simple question and somehow end up having a full conversation about it an hour later.

So, take a look around! You might discover something interesting here, learn something new, or find a topic that sparks your own curiosity.

And if you happen to find a typo, a bug, or an unfinished thought — that’s simply proof that the learning is still in progress. ✨

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you enjoy exploring this little corner of my world!

Most of what you'll find here lives somewhere between bioinformatics, computer science, research, and whatever niche topic my brain has decided to hyperfixate on this week.

It's a collection of projects, research work, course notes, coding adventures, random thoughts and occasional proof that you can, in fact, learn almost anything with enough curiosity, persistence, and an unreasonable number of browser tabs.

I don't really believe learning happens in a neat, step-by-step process.

Sometimes it's organized notes, clear goals, and a solid plan.

Sometimes it's 2 a.m., seventeen tabs open, three different tutorials saying three different things, and me searching:

"Why is my code broken?"
followed immediately by
"Wait... why is it working now?"

Either way, that's usually where the interesting stuff happens.

So feel free to look around.

You might learn something.

Honestly, there's a decent chance I'm learning it too.
