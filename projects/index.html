About Me

Hi! Welcome 👋

This website is basically a public record of me learning things, building things, breaking things, fixing the things I broke, and occasionally pretending that all of this was part of the plan.

Most of the content here revolves around bioinformatics, computer science, research, and whatever rabbit hole my curiosity decides to disappear into next. You'll find projects, experiments, coding adventures, course notes, and proof that determination can sometimes compensate for a concerning amount of confusion.

I'm a firm believer that learning doesn't happen in a straight line. Sometimes it looks like careful planning.

Sometimes it looks like twenty browser tabs, three cups of coffee, and a Google search that starts with "Why is my code..."

Either way, if you're here, feel free to look around.

You might learn something.

I probably did too. 🙂
